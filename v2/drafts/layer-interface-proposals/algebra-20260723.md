# Layer interfaces — Proposal: layers as algebras, implementations as interpreters

Status: DRAFT (alternative standardization proposal; peer to
`docs/spec/layer-interfaces.md`, Proposal A, and the three sibling
drafts under `v2/drafts/layer-interface-proposals/`)
Bias: a layer is its equations.

`docs/spec/endpoints/{channels,contacts,screening,tasks}.md`;

## Summary

The standardized artifact per layer is an **algebra**: a signature of
operation symbols over a fixed set of payload sorts, plus a body of
**equations** — laws stated as equalities between two programs. The
equations are not a validation pass bolted onto an interface; they *are*
the layer's definition. The router pipeline, the channel core, and the
case-study benches are **programs** — terms written once against the
algebra signatures. Production, the testbed, and in-memory tests are
**interpreters** — assignments of an Effect-valued meaning to each
operation symbol. "Guarantees flow up" becomes **algebra layering**: L3's
algebra extends L2's signature and its durable-then-deliver equation
quantifies over L2's delivery operation. "Configuration flows down"
becomes **interpreter parameters**: a norm bundle, an operator key, or an
L7 revocation is an argument to the interpreter that realizes a lower
algebra, never a lower operation reaching up. The testbed decision
(`docs/decisions/20260723-eval-plane-is-testbed.md`) becomes literally
**one algebra, two interpreters, equations preserved**.

Where this diverges structurally from all four priors. (1) Against
**Proposal A** (service catalog): A's unit is a *service* — a
`Context.Tag` with request/response methods — and the layering rule is
recovered *after the fact* as an acyclicity property of the Tag graph.
Here the unit is an *equational theory*; layering is *signature
extension* declared at definition time (`𝒜₃ extends 𝒜₂`), and a
guarantee is an equation, not a method. (2) Against **minimal-ports**,
the nearest neighbor: its unit is a *port* (a swap axis) and its laws are
*hand-written conformance properties over port bindings*; there are five
ports because there are five swap axes, and each law is a property
someone wrote. Here the unit is a *layer's algebra*; there are five
algebras because five layers carry operations, the swap is **not a unit**
(it is two interpreters of one algebra), and the checking machinery — a
program generator and an observational-equivalence checker — is
**derived from the reified signature value**, so laws are not written per
property. (3) Against **schema-first** (payloads as the source of truth,
boundaries as codecs) and **stream-journal** (one journal, everything a
fold): both standardize *data shape* — a schema at rest, or a `Stream`
read — whereas an algebra standardizes *behaviour over operation
sequences*; the schema and the journal are, in this frame, one
interpreter's carrier, checked by replaying generated terms, not by
decoding a payload or folding a stream.

Count: **five algebras** (L1 identity, L2 delivery, L3 transactional
messaging, L5 personal trust, L7 institutional trust), **31 layer
equations + 3 cross-cutting laws + 2 homomorphism conditions** (L4 tasks
and L6 oversight mint no operation; L8 is open). Of the 34
equations/laws, ~12 are discharged at compile time (the violation is
unrepresentable in the signature), ~16 fall out mechanically as
property tests replayed from the reified signature, and ~6 are
conformance-suite laws needing a second interpreter or a case-study
program.

## Modules

Conceptual units, not a package count; the physical component-to-package
map is the v0 plan's W1 (`v2/wire`, `v2/identity`, `v2/server`,
`v2/plane`, `v2/channel`, `v2/testbed-plane`, `v2/cli`, `v2/conformance`)
and is inherited unchanged. Four units define the standard, two realize
it per region, one derives the suite.

1. **`algebra/kernel`** — the meta-vocabulary and the whole novelty.
   Public surface: `Sort`, `OpSymbol`, `Signature` (a signature reified
   as a *value*), `Program` (a closed term of the free algebra over a
   signature), `Interpreter` (a Σ-algebra whose carrier is Effect-valued),
   `Equation` (a pair of open terms plus a doc citation and a discharge
   kind), `LayerAlgebra` (signature + equations + the lower algebra it
   extends), and the **derived** combinators `programGen`, `runProgram`,
   `checkEquation`, `checkEquivalence`. These combinators are
   `<S extends Signature>`-polymorphic: the generator and the
   equivalence checker are produced *from* a signature, never written per
   law. Dependencies: `effect` (Effect, Stream, Context, Layer, Brand),
   `fast-check` (the target of the derived generator). Imports no other
   unit. This is the kernel; everything else consumes it.

2. **`algebra/wire`** — the **sorts**: the decided payload floor as
   branded/opaque types (`AgentId`, `PrincipalRef`, `Card`, `Envelope`,
   `Body`, `EncodedFrame`, `ProtocolVersion`, `ConversationId`,
   `Position`, `TranscriptRecord`, `LifecycleEntry`, `CollectiveUnit`,
   `Refusal`, `Standing`, `GateVerdict`), plus the two deliberately-open
   unions (`CollectiveUnit`, `LifecycleEntry`). A sort is the type an
   interpreter's carrier realizes; the wire never depends on the kernel.
   Dependencies: `effect` (Schema, Brand). Home `v2/wire`.

3. **`algebra/layers`** — the **five `LayerAlgebra` values**: `Identity`
   (𝒜₁), `Delivery` (𝒜₂), `Transactional` (𝒜₃), `Trust` (𝒜₅),
   `Institutional` (𝒜₇), each a reified signature plus its equations,
   declaring the algebra it extends. Also the two homomorphism
   statements: L4 tasks (a norm bundle is an interpreter *presentation*;
   same-version agreement is two endpoints instantiating 𝒜₅ from one
   presentation) and L6 oversight (`evidence` is a derived term; the
   monitor's interpretation of `open` equals the recipient's). L8 has no
   value. Dependencies: `algebra/kernel`, `algebra/wire`. This unit is a
   two-layer stack over kernel + wire.

4. **Region interpreters (production).** `RouterInterp` realizes 𝒜₂/𝒜₃/𝒜₇
   over a durable-transcript carrier (home `v2/server`); `EndpointInterp`
   realizes 𝒜₁/𝒜₃-client-side/𝒜₅ over a channel carrier (home
   `v2/channel`). An interpreter is a `Layer` providing the algebra's
   `Context.Tag`; algebra layering is `Layer` requirement (the 𝒜₃ Layer
   requires the 𝒜₂ tag). The CLI (`v2/cli`) is a program over 𝒜₇ and the
   𝒜₃ read operations, holding no interpreter of its own. Dependencies:
   `algebra/layers`.

5. **Testbed interpreter.** `TestbedInterp` — a **second interpreter of
   the identical 𝒜₂/𝒜₃ signature** whose carrier adds envelope-level
   observation and bounded fault injection, and nothing else. The swap in
   `data-plane.md` invariant 11 is substituting this Layer for
   `RouterInterp`'s delivery Layer. Home `v2/testbed-plane`. Dependencies:
   `algebra/layers`.

6. **`algebra/conformance`** — runs the derived machinery: for each
   `LayerAlgebra`, `programGen` yields op-sequences, `checkEquation`
   replays each property/conformance equation against an interpreter, and
   `checkEquivalence` runs one swap-gate call `RouterInterp ≈ TestbedInterp`.
   The two-consumer falsification is two programs (bench-shaped,
   arena-shaped) run under `EndpointInterp`. Home `v2/conformance` (W8-
   owned). Dependencies: `algebra/kernel`, `algebra/layers`, the
   interpreters as subjects.

Folder shape: kernel and wire are independent leaves; `algebra/layers`
is a two-layer stack above them; the interpreters are a tree of peers
(router, endpoint, testbed) none importing another; conformance is the
edge leaf consuming kernel + layers + subjects. Visible from the
directory listing: meta below, algebras in the middle, interpreters
beside, suite at the edge.

**Effect realization (standardized).** The algebras are `Context.Tag`
services and the interpreters are `Layer`s — so algebra layering is a
`Layer` dependency and the guarantees-flow-up graph property is checkable
over the `Layer` graph exactly as the other proposals get it. The
tagless-final alternative — an algebra as `interface Alg<F> { ship: (u) =>
F<Position> }` and a program as `<F>(alg: Alg<F>) => F<…>` — buys nothing
extra *here*, because Effect fixes `F = Effect`, so the universally-
quantified `F` collapses to "an interface whose methods return `Effect`,"
which is the `Context.Tag` value anyway. What tagless-final's *spirit*
does buy — a program as a term over abstract operations, independent of
any interpreter — this proposal keeps, but realizes it as the **reified
`Signature` value + `Program` term** rather than a `forall F` type,
because a value is what the derived generator and equivalence checker can
consume. So the standard is: **`Context.Tag` per algebra + `Layer` per
interpreter for realization; a reified `Signature` for conformance.** The
reified signature is the artifact no sibling proposal has.

## Interfaces

TypeScript with typed error channels; no bodies (architecture).
`Effect<A, E>` elides the `R` requirement, stated per interpreter in
Modules. Equations sit beside each algebra in the notation defined below.

**Notation.** `p ; q` = do `p`, then `q` (Effect sequencing). `⟦op⟧_I` =
op's meaning under interpreter `I`. `p ≈ q` = `p` and `q` are
**observationally equal** — projecting the observable sorts (positions,
delivered records, verdicts, cards) yields the same result under every
interpreter, never comparing interpreter-internal carrier state. `≜` =
definitional. `refuse` = the failure inhabitant of the `Effect` error
channel (`Refusal`). An **equation** carries a discharge kind: **(C)**
compile-time — the violation is unrepresentable in the signature, zero
test execution; **(P)** property — a term-pair replayed by `checkEquation`
over `programGen`; **(S)** conformance-suite — needs a second interpreter
or a case-study program.

### Kernel — the meta-vocabulary (`algebra/kernel`)

```ts
import type { Effect } from "effect/Effect";
import type { Brand } from "effect/Brand";
import type { Arbitrary } from "fast-check";

/** A sort names a payload type the signature ranges over (a wire noun). */
export type Sort = string & Brand.Brand<"Sort">;

/** An operation symbol: its name, the sorts of its arguments, its result sort.
 *  Reified as a VALUE so the generator and checker can read it. */
export interface OpSymbol<
  Args extends readonly Sort[] = readonly Sort[],
  Result extends Sort = Sort,
> {
  readonly name: string;
  readonly args: Args;
  readonly result: Result;
  /** True iff the result sort is observable (may be projected by `≈`). */
  readonly observable: boolean;
}

/** A signature reified as a value: the sorts, the operation symbols, and the
 *  lower signature this one extends (algebra layering, declared here). */
export interface Signature {
  readonly sorts: readonly Sort[];
  readonly ops: readonly OpSymbol[];
  readonly extends: Signature | null;
}

/** A closed term of the free (initial) algebra over Σ: a well-sorted
 *  operation-sequence. `programGen` produces these; interpreters run them. */
export type Program<S extends Signature> = readonly TermNode[] &
  Brand.Brand<"Program">;
export interface TermNode {
  readonly op: string; // an OpSymbol.name in the signature or a lower one
  readonly args: readonly (TermRef | Literal)[]; // refs bind earlier results by sort
}
export type TermRef = { readonly _tag: "Ref"; readonly index: number; readonly sort: Sort };
export type Literal = { readonly _tag: "Lit"; readonly sort: Sort; readonly value: unknown };

/** An interpreter IS a Σ-algebra: a carrier per sort plus an Effect-valued
 *  meaning per operation symbol. `Refusal` is the uniform failure inhabitant. */
export interface Interpreter<S extends Signature> {
  readonly signature: S;
  /** apply the named op to already-evaluated carrier arguments. */
  readonly apply: (
    op: string,
    args: readonly unknown[],
  ) => Effect<unknown, Refusal>;
}

/** An equation: two OPEN terms (over variables) asserted observationally
 *  equal, a doc citation (unbuildable without one), and a discharge kind. */
export interface Equation<S extends Signature> {
  readonly name: string;
  readonly cite: DocCitation; // { doc: string; ref: string } — spec + invariant/section
  readonly lhs: OpenTerm<S>;
  readonly rhs: OpenTerm<S>;
  readonly kind: "compile" | "property" | "conformance";
}
export interface DocCitation { readonly doc: string; readonly ref: string }
export type OpenTerm<S extends Signature> = TermNode & Brand.Brand<"OpenTerm">;

/** A layer's standardized artifact: signature + equations + the algebra it
 *  extends. This is the unit the whole proposal is organized around. */
export interface LayerAlgebra<S extends Signature> {
  readonly layer: "L1" | "L2" | "L3" | "L5" | "L7";
  readonly signature: S;
  readonly equations: readonly Equation<S>[];
  readonly extendsAlgebra: LayerAlgebra<Signature> | null;
}

// ── Derived from the signature value — NOT written per law ──────────────────

/** Generate well-sorted closed terms of T(Σ): random operation-sequences whose
 *  arg sorts are satisfied by earlier results. Derived purely from `sig`. */
export declare const programGen: <S extends Signature>(sig: S) => Arbitrary<Program<S>>;

/** Fold a term with an interpreter, yielding the observable projection. */
export declare const runProgram: <S extends Signature>(
  interp: Interpreter<S>,
  program: Program<S>,
) => Effect<Observation, Refusal>;
export interface Observation { readonly byObservableSort: ReadonlyMap<Sort, readonly unknown[]> }

/** Check one property/conformance equation: instantiate its open terms from
 *  `programGen`, run both sides under `interp`, assert `≈`. Failures are values. */
export declare const checkEquation: <S extends Signature>(
  interp: Interpreter<S>,
  eq: Equation<S>,
) => Effect<PropertyResult, PropertyFailure>;

/** The swap gate: two interpreters of ONE signature are observationally equal
 *  over `programGen(sig)`. This is `data-plane.md` inv. 11 as one call. */
export declare const checkEquivalence: <S extends Signature>(
  a: Interpreter<S>,
  b: Interpreter<S>,
) => Effect<PropertyResult, PropertyFailure>;

export interface PropertyResult { readonly _tag: "Holds"; readonly cases: number }
export interface PropertyFailure { readonly _tag: "Counterexample"; readonly program: Program<Signature> }
```

### Sorts — the payload floor (`algebra/wire`)

```ts
import type { Brand } from "effect/Brand";

export type AgentId = string & Brand.Brand<"AgentId">;            // registry-minted, opaque, survives rotation
export type PrincipalRef = string & Brand.Brand<"PrincipalRef">; // opaque linkage (depth open)
export type ProtocolVersion = string & Brand.Brand<"ProtocolVersion">; // CalVer; exact match, missing segments = 0
export type ConversationId = string & Brand.Brand<"ConversationId">;   // client-minted, collision-free by size
export type Position = bigint & Brand.Brand<"Position">;         // store-assigned; NEVER a field of any frame sort
export type Body = Uint8Array & Brand.Brand<"Body">;             // opaque; never read below L4
export type EncodedFrame = Uint8Array & Brand.Brand<"EncodedFrame">;   // the attributed unit; byte-exact at every hop
export type Card = Uint8Array & Brand.Brand<"Card">;             // X.509 container; self-attesting

/** Carrier-readable view. Routing and admission read ONLY these fields; the
 *  absence of Position from this type is invariant 7, made structural. */
export interface Envelope {
  readonly sender: AgentId;
  readonly conversation: ConversationId;
  readonly protocol: ProtocolVersion;
}
/** The result of a successful `open`: the readable view plus the exact bytes. */
export interface Attributed {
  readonly envelope: Envelope;
  readonly principal: PrincipalRef;
  readonly frame: EncodedFrame;
}
/** A committed record: the frame byte-exact PLUS the store's position (outside the frame). */
export interface TranscriptRecord { readonly frame: EncodedFrame; readonly position: Position }

/** OPEN union: v0 has exactly START/MEMBER_ADD/LEAVE. #765 widens it, forcing an
 *  exhaustive-match compile error at every fold and every interpreter. */
export type LifecycleEntry =
  | { readonly _tag: "START"; readonly founder: AgentId }
  | { readonly _tag: "MEMBER_ADD"; readonly member: AgentId }
  | { readonly _tag: "LEAVE"; readonly member: AgentId };

/** OPEN union: v0 has exactly MULTICAST. Op-set growth (#765) is a new union arm
 *  (equivalently a new operation symbol), never a new method on any interface. */
export type CollectiveUnit = { readonly _tag: "MULTICAST"; readonly frame: EncodedFrame };
export type TranscriptUnit = CollectiveUnit | LifecycleEntry;

/** The one interim refusal value. `cause` is an OPAQUE brand, not a closed union:
 *  closing it would bind the failure taxonomy (register 8, open). */
export type RefusalCause = string & Brand.Brand<"RefusalCause">;
export interface Refusal { readonly _tag: "Refusal"; readonly cause: RefusalCause }

/** L5 posture and verdict. Verdict rides the SUCCESS channel (never a Refusal). */
export type Standing =
  | { readonly _tag: "Allow" }
  | { readonly _tag: "Deny" }
  | { readonly _tag: "Limit"; readonly limits: Limits };
export type GateVerdict =
  | { readonly _tag: "Admit" }
  | { readonly _tag: "AdmitUnderLimits"; readonly limits: Limits }
  | { readonly _tag: "Refuse" };
export type Limits = ReadonlyArray<string & Brand.Brand<"Limit">>; // endpoint-defined, opaque
```

### 𝒜₁ — Identity algebra (L1)

Base algebra; extends nothing. Carrier sorts: `AgentId`, `Card`,
`Envelope`, `Body`, `EncodedFrame`, `Attributed`.

```ts
import type { Effect } from "effect/Effect";
import { Context } from "effect/Context";

export interface Identity {
  /** Attribute a draft at emit; frames leave already attributable (identity.md inv. 2). */
  readonly seal: (envelope: Envelope, body: Body) => Effect<EncodedFrame, Refusal>;
  /** Verify offline from the frame plus the sender's card alone; same shape under
   *  interim and target bindings (identity.md → One shape, two attribution bindings). */
  readonly open: (frame: EncodedFrame, card: Card) => Effect<Attributed, Refusal>;
  /** Resolve an identity's current card; the card is the directory entry. */
  readonly resolve: (id: AgentId) => Effect<Card, Refusal>;
}
export class IdentityTag extends Context.Tag("moltzap/v2/alg/Identity")<IdentityTag, Identity>() {}
```

Equations (the interim and target bindings are two interpreters; every
equation holds under both):

```
E1.1 verify-after-author  (P):  open(seal(e, b), resolve(e.sender)) ≈ Attributed(e, ·, seal(e, b))
        — verify-after-author is the identity on the attributed view.
E1.2 offline              (C):  open : (EncodedFrame, Card) → Attributed  — no requirement on a live
        sender; the signature admits no round-trip argument. (identity.md → Verification duties)
E1.3 tamper-refuse        (P):  ∀ δ altering envelope or body,  open(δ(seal(e,b)), c) ≈ refuse.
E1.4 resolve-after-mint   (P):  resolve(agentOf(mint(pk, pr))) ≈ card binding pk and pr.   (see 𝒜₇)
E1.5 revocation-by-omission (C+P): there is NO revoke operation; once 𝒜₇ ceases to vouch,
        resolve(id) ≈ refuse — observed at the next resolve. (identity.md; single-credential)
E1.6 attests-who-only     (C):  no operation of 𝒜₁ takes or returns a trust/intent sort.
```

### 𝒜₂ — Delivery algebra (L2)

Extends 𝒜₁. Carrier adds a per-conversation ordered log. This is the raw
ordered-multicast primitive; it does not own the commit (that is 𝒜₃).

```ts
export interface Delivery {
  /** Envelope-only admission: attribution verifies, sender exists and is active, sender is a
   *  member — or a genesis to a fresh id. Reads Envelope, NEVER Body. Refuses before durability. */
  readonly admit: (frame: EncodedFrame) => Effect<Attributed, Refusal>;
  /** One-way ordered push of committed records, resumable from an endpoint-owned Position.
   *  Best-effort; NEVER the source of truth; NEVER a response path. */
  readonly deliveries: (
    conversation: ConversationId,
    from: Position,
  ) => Stream<TranscriptRecord, Refusal>;
}
export class DeliveryTag extends Context.Tag("moltzap/v2/alg/Delivery")<DeliveryTag, Delivery>() {}
```

Equations:

```
E2.1 total-order          (P):  ∀ members m, n and records r, s delivered to both,
        order_m(r, s) = order_n(r, s).  (data-plane.md inv. 3)
E2.2 attribution-preserve (C+P): open(deliveries(...)_i) ≈ open(shipped_i) — delivery neither
        mints, alters, nor strips attribution; EncodedFrame is carried byte-exact.  (inv. 2, 13)
E2.3 content-blind        (C):  admit : (EncodedFrame) → Attributed reads Envelope only; no
        operation of 𝒜₂ takes a Body argument.  (data-plane.md inv. 1)
E2.4 one-way              (C):  deliveries returns a Stream; no operation accepts a reply on the
        delivery path; a response is a fresh 𝒜₃.ship.  (data-plane.md inv. 14)
E2.5 equivocation-robust  (P):  one ship yields one frame image; ∀ members, deliveries of it are
        byte-identical (holds by construction: single carrier copy).  (data-plane.md inv. 7)
E2.6 recover-equals-read  (P):  deliveries(c, p) ≈ 𝒜₃.read(c, p) as multisets of records —
        resuming at a Position is observationally never-disconnecting.  (control-plane.md g.4)
```

### 𝒜₃ — Transactional messaging algebra (L3)

**Extends 𝒜₂** — this is where "L3's algebra is defined over L2's
operations" is literal: `ship` is defined as `append` then 𝒜₂-delivery,
and the durable-then-deliver equation quantifies over `deliveries`.
Carrier = the durable per-conversation transcript.

```ts
export interface Transactional {
  /** Ship one transactional unit: append then best-effort deliver. Position returns ONLY after
   *  durability; every refusal precedes durability. `ship` is DERIVED (see E3.2), shown for the
   *  program surface. */
  readonly ship: (unit: TranscriptUnit) => Effect<Position, Refusal>;
  /** Append one unit as one transaction; commit-time contiguous Position; after durability. */
  readonly append: (
    conversation: ConversationId,
    unit: TranscriptUnit,
  ) => Effect<Position, Refusal>;
  /** Genesis: atomic iff the frame's conversation id is unused; reuse refuses with NO side effect. */
  readonly appendGenesis: (frame: EncodedFrame) => Effect<Position, Refusal>;
  /** Contiguous ordered window, byte-exact records, gated by the entitlement predicate. */
  readonly read: (
    conversation: ConversationId,
    from: Position,
    scope: Scope,
  ) => Effect<readonly TranscriptRecord[], Refusal>;
  /** Membership DERIVED from lifecycle entries at or before a position; no stored membership. */
  readonly membershipAt: (
    conversation: ConversationId,
    at: Position,
  ) => Effect<readonly AgentId[], Refusal>;
  /** Observe admission before generation: agreement precedes generation (data-plane.md inv. 5).
   *  Returns a guarantee-level token; the PCC instrument and its carriage live in the carrier. */
  readonly awaitAdmission: (conversation: ConversationId) => Effect<AdmittedTurn, Refusal>;
}
/** Entitlement predicate carried into read (the L3 scope seam). v0: membership-only. Not an op. */
export type Scope = (record: TranscriptRecord) => Effect<boolean, never>;
/** Guarantee-level admission token. No lease, socket, or connection is named. */
export interface AdmittedTurn { readonly conversation: ConversationId; readonly at: Position }
export class TransactionalTag extends Context.Tag("moltzap/v2/alg/Transactional")<
  TransactionalTag, Transactional
>() {}
```

`initiate` and `replyGuard` are **derived terms**, not primitive
operations (E3.7, E3.9). Membership is a derivation (E3.6).

```
E3.1 append-read          (P):  read(c, pos(append(c, u)))  ≈  [record(u)]
        — append-then-read-from-position is the appended record. The core equation.
E3.2 durable-then-deliver (P):  ship(u)  ≜  append(c, u) ; bestEffort(𝒜₂.deliveries)
        and  ship(u) returns Position  ⟹  the record is durable  ⟹  deliverable.
        No 𝒜₂.deliveries item precedes its append commit.  (data-plane.md inv. 4; g.1)
E3.3 collective-atomic    (C+P): append takes ONE TranscriptUnit; read never returns a partial
        unit — a collective commits as one transactional unit.  (control-plane.md g.9)
E3.4 immutability         (C):  𝒜₃ has no update/delete/rewrite operation; the transcript is a
        free (append-only) structure.  (control-plane.md g.6)
E3.5 genesis-freshness    (P):  appendGenesis(f) with conv(f) used  ≈  refuse, and read(conv(f))
        is unchanged — reuse refuses with no side effect.  (lifecycle-rides-l3)
E3.6 membership-derived   (C+P): membershipAt(c, p)  ≈  fold(step, {LifecycleEntry ≤ p});
        no membership-write operation exists.  (data-plane.md inv. 8; storage g.5)
E3.7 initiate-derived     (C):  initiate(members, body)  ≜  fresh ConversationId ; 𝒜₁.seal(START) ;
        appendGenesis — a term, not a primitive; NO create operation.  (lifecycle-rides-l3)
E3.8 turn-before-generate (P):  in any program, awaitAdmission(c) precedes every append attributed
        to that turn — agreement precedes generation.  (data-plane.md inv. 5)
E3.9 reply-guard-one-shot (C+P): replyGuard(t)  ≜  at most one ship per AdmittedTurn t; a second
        is held.  (data-plane.md → Implementation notes: at most one admitted turn)
```

### 𝒜₅ — Personal-trust algebra (L5)

Extends 𝒜₃ and 𝒜₁, endpoint-local. Screening is an *endomorphism* on the
delivery/send flow that never touches the record — the homomorphism
condition E5.2 is the load-bearing law.

### The free-encoding trick, concretely

The generator, the runner, and the equivalence checker are
`<S extends Signature>`-polymorphic — they read the reified `Signature`
value and never see a specific law. Standing up a layer's conformance is:
supply the signature value, the equation term-pairs with citations, and
the interpreters. Nothing per-property is hand-written.

```ts
// The Transactional signature as a VALUE (abbreviated). Widening the op set (#765) adds an
// OpSymbol here and a union arm to CollectiveUnit; programGen and every interpreter then must
// handle it — an additive, localized change, never a new interface method.
export declare const TransactionalSig: Signature; // ops: ship, append, appendGenesis, read, membershipAt, awaitAdmission; extends DeliverySig

// Conformance for the swap gate (data-plane.md inv. 11) is ONE call, and the op-sequences it
// replays are GENERATED from the signature — not a hand-written list of laws:
export declare const swapGate: Effect<PropertyResult, PropertyFailure>;
//   ≡  checkEquivalence(RouterInterp, TestbedInterp)   over programGen(TransactionalSig)

// A single equation becomes a property with no bespoke body:
export declare const appendRead: Equation<typeof TransactionalSig>; // E3.1, cite control-plane.md g.2
//   checkEquation(RouterInterp, appendRead)  instantiates lhs/rhs from programGen, runs both, asserts ≈
```

This is the concrete difference from minimal-ports: there, each law is a
property someone writes against a port binding; here, `checkEquation` and
`checkEquivalence` are *derived from Σ*, so the suite that proves a layer
is generated from the same value that defines the layer.

## Errors

Typed channels only; no throw crosses an operation. Three tiers, aligned
with the prior convergence.

- **`Refusal` — the one wire value.** Every operation an endpoint can
  observe across the wire fails with `Refusal`, whose `cause` is an
  **opaque brand**, not a closed union. Closing it would bind the failure
  taxonomy (`data-plane.md` register item 8, open). This is the single
  union the design deliberately leaves open, by construction: no
  operation returns a `RefusalCause` structure a consumer could switch on.

- **Interpreter-internal typed errors.** An interpreter's carrier may use
  a discriminated union internally (`IdInUse`, `NotAMember`,
  `InvalidCursor`, `VersionMismatch`) and project each to one `Refusal` at
  the operation boundary; conformance asserts the *effect* (the op did not
  take effect), never the *shape*. These never appear in an algebra
  signature.

- **`GateVerdict` is not an error.** The 𝒜₅ gate returns `GateVerdict` in
  the **success** channel: `Admit | AdmitUnderLimits | Refuse`, a closed
  exhaustive union, agent-local, never on the wire (E5.4, prior
  convergence 3). A refused frame is withheld from the runtime while the
  record stays in the transcript (E5.2).

Exhaustiveness. Every closed union an interpreter matches — the internal
error unions, `GateVerdict`, and the *known* arms of `LifecycleEntry` /
`CollectiveUnit` — ends in a `never`-typed default (`absurd`). The two
**open** sorts (`CollectiveUnit`, `LifecycleEntry`) are open by
construction: adding an arm is adding an operation symbol to the affected
signature, which (a) is a compile error at every interpreter's `apply`
until handled and (b) regenerates `programGen` to exercise it — so #765
widening is additive and mechanically surfaced, binding nothing now.

## Open questions

Each carries a recommended default and an escalation target. Every
chartered or register-open item is left open **by construction** — the
mechanism is named in the second sentence.

1. **Does the algebra layering graduate from a recorded standard to a
   decision record binding v2 idiom?** Same question A, minimal-ports, and
   schema-first raise. *Recommended default:* keep it a recorded standard
   (Context.Tag per algebra, Layer per interpreter, reified Signature for
   conformance); raise a decision record when the first implementation PR
   would deviate. Escalation: `docs/decisions/`.

2. **The op set beyond v0 MULTICAST (#765).** Left open by construction:
   `CollectiveUnit` is an open union with one arm and the op set is a set
   of operation symbols; #765 adds an arm/symbol, forcing an exhaustive-
   match compile error at every interpreter and regenerating `programGen`.
   *Recommended default:* v0 ships exactly MULTICAST + START/MEMBER_ADD/
   LEAVE; no operation is added per op. Escalation: charter #765.

3. **Turn-signal carriage (#765).** Left open by construction:
   `awaitAdmission` returns a guarantee-level `AdmittedTurn`; the PCC
   instrument and its wire signal are 𝒜₃ interpreter carrier state, named
   by no operation symbol. *Recommended default:* the interim signal rides
   inside `RouterInterp` as non-normative mechanism; the charter replaces
   it without touching the 𝒜₃ signature. Escalation: charter #765.

4. **Data-plane wire shape (`data-plane.md` Q10).** Left open by
   construction: `ship`/`deliveries`/`admit` are in-process operations;
   the wire is inside the interpreter carrier, named by no symbol (prior
   convergence 4). *Recommended default:* bind no framing, feed scope, or
   cursor encoding in any signature; a wire choice rewrites an interpreter,
   not an algebra. `Position` is the only resume token either way.
   Escalation: `docs/spec/data-plane.md`.

5. **Key model (register 5).** Left open by construction: 𝒜₁ is
   binding-neutral; interim (sign the request) and target (sign the frame)
   are two interpreters of one signature, and every 𝒜₁ equation holds
   under both. *Recommended default:* the migration is an interpreter
   swap, invisible to every program; no rotation/revocation/per-frame-
   signing operation appears. Escalation: `docs/spec/identity.md`.

6. **Refusal wire shape (register 8).** Left open by construction:
   `Refusal.cause` is an opaque brand, the single union the design does
   not close. *Recommended default:* conformance asserts refusal *effect*
   (the op did not take effect; the record is withheld from attention),
   never shape or code; the register-8 taxonomy widens `RefusalCause`
   without changing any signature. Escalation: register 8.

7. **Strict-decode normativity (register 9).** Left open by construction:
   decoding at the frame boundary is an interpreter's carrier option, in
   no algebra signature and no equation. *Recommended default:* strict
   decode is an implementation posture asserted by no spec-partition
   property; any strict-decode test is a non-normative pin, machine-
   prevented from gating acceptance. Escalation: register 9.

8. **Monitor access (register 3).** Left open by construction: L6 mints
   no operation; `evidence` is the derived term `map(open, read)` under a
   `Scope` wider than membership — the seam is the `read` predicate, the
   wider scope unbuilt (**H6**). *Recommended default:* v0 mints no
   monitor principal and no third caller; a monitor runs through member-
   scoped `Scope`, and register-3 later supplies a wider predicate value,
   not a new operation. Escalation: register 3.

9. **Cursor persistence (`channels.md` Q2).** Left open: `deliveries`/
   `read` take a `Position`; what the endpoint must durably keep across
   restarts is a spec deliverable. *Recommended default:* the operations
   bind no persistence; `EndpointInterp` decides durability under the
   W6.S2 spec item (peek-then-commit is the candidate). Escalation:
   `docs/spec/endpoints/channels.md`.

10. **Point-in-time reads vs. re-derivation cost (the accepted trade-off,
    shared with stream-journal).** `membershipAt` is a derived fold, not a
    stored O(1) lookup (**E3.6**). *Recommended default:* an interpreter
    may materialize the hot fold in its carrier (a `SubscriptionRef` of
    conversation state) updated as records commit, so a read is a ref-get
    and only a cold conversation re-folds; this is interpreter freedom the
    algebra and its equations never see. Escalation: W4/W6 code review.

11. **Is 𝒜₅ screening one algebra or a family?** Screening is one algebra
    with a pluggable presentation (the norm bundle + contacts flow down as
    interpreter parameters, **H4**); the conformance goal is
    *expressibility* (arena and bench rulesets both instantiate 𝒜₅), not
    *equivalence* (their interpreters are intentionally different, so
    `checkEquivalence` is not run on 𝒜₅). *Recommended default:* keep 𝒜₅
    one algebra; a shared skill-distributable firewall vocabulary
    (`contacts.md` rec. 6) would add equations, not a new algebra.
    Escalation: `docs/spec/endpoints/screening.md`.
