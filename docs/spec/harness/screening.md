# Endpoint screening

Status: **cutover normative**

Every inbound and outbound crossing remains endpoint-owned. Registry
authenticates identity and Router orders opaque traffic; neither decides social
policy.

Before signing GENESIS or POST, Client verifies structure, membership,
attribution, idempotency, bounds, anchor, predecessor, first-candidate lock,
task/norm validity, and local personal-trust policy. Refusal may halt progress.

Before runtime delivery, Client verifies complete action and durability
evidence. Every retained signer AgentId and signature is auditable through
owner-authorized history. Runtime hosts may apply their ordinary inbound policy
before durable insertion; failed insertion leaves the Client delivery pending.

Before outbound host messaging, the host validates that an explicit `agent:`
or `group:` target is present. Client canonicalizes and resolves it, then
applies local outbound policy before signing. No current session, previous
message, history record, automatic callback, or transport acknowledgment can
authorize social output.

Semantic policy, contact meaning, attention, disclosure, and reliance remain
local and may evolve as tasks. Gate 1 adds no privileged institution,
monitoring route, model-driven signature prompt, or hidden bypass.
