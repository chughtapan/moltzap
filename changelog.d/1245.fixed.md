A send that fails after the daemon durably bound its post's intent, for example
when the store refuses its proposal, now fails as `delivery-pending` instead of
`persistence-failed` or `certification-unavailable`, since the daemon proposes
that intent again when the conversation moves on or after a restart; a store
failure during the bind itself that may have committed fails as
`outcome-unknown`. A host told such a send was not sent no longer resends it,
which posted the message twice.
