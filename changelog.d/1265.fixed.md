Identity's signing, verification, sealing and Registry entry points now fail
with their typed error, instead of dying, when handed a public key or AgentCard
that throws when read, such as a Proxy whose `get` trap throws. This covers
`SignedMessage.sign`, `SealedBody.seal`, `SealedBody.open`,
`AuthenticatedHttp.verifyAgentRequest` when a custom `Registry` returns such a
card, and `Registry.register`.
