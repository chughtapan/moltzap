`SignedMessage.sign`, `SealedBody.seal`, `SealedBody.open`, `Registry.register`,
and `AuthenticatedHttp.verifyAgentRequest` when a custom `Registry` returns the
card, now fail with their typed error instead of dying when given a public key
or AgentCard that throws on read, such as a Proxy whose `get` trap throws.
`AuthenticatedHttp.verifyAgentRequest` also refuses a lookup result whose card
this package did not verify.
