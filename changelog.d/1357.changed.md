An uncertain norm judgment no longer holds a message. For a norm rule, an
applicability, expectations or conformance answer below `minimumProbability` or
`unresolved` admits the message, records a `pass` finding with reason `uncertain` and the judge's
choice and probability, and reaches the agent as guidance with the rule's
instruction: on the admitted incoming item, or in an admitted send's new
`guidance` result field, which the native, MCP and OpenClaw send results carry.
A norm whose applicability is uncertain is still judged, and conformance judges
only the settled expectations. Uncertainty about a safety or autonomy rule,
its applicability included, and a failed judge still hold.
