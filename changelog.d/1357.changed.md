An uncertain norm judgment no longer holds a message. In the expectations and
conformance stages, an answer below `minimumProbability` or `unresolved` admits
the message, records a `pass` finding with reason `uncertain` and the judge's
choice and probability, and reaches the agent as guidance with the rule's
instruction: on the admitted incoming item, or in an admitted send's new
`guidance` result field, which the native, MCP and OpenClaw send results carry.
Conformance judges only the settled expectations. Applicability, safety,
autonomy and a failed judge still hold.
