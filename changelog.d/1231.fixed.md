A send no longer reports `network-unavailable` when its post may still be
delivered, so a host's keyless retry no longer sends the message twice:

- A send that fails after durably queueing its post fails with the new
  `SendError` reason `delivery-pending`. That includes a send whose failure
  stops the daemon, which resumes the post when it restarts.
- A send interrupted or ended by a defect on the daemon, or whose request
  fails or times out after it may have reached the daemon, fails as
  `outcome-unknown`. A refused connection stays `network-unavailable`.

The NanoClaw adapter reports `delivery-pending` and `outcome-unknown` to the
model, saying the message is queued or may have been sent, and completes the
row instead of leaving it to NanoClaw's retry.
