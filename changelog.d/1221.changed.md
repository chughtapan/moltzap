Each member now sends the action-certified record it assembles once and no
longer sends a copy of the certified record, which every member assembles
from the durability votes it receives. A post costs at most `1 + 3n` outer
messages instead of `1 + 4n`. Measured in the certification harness with
sealed bodies, the bytes each member receives per post fall by 50% at 4
members and 58% at 32 members.
