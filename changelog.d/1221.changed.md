Each member now sends its own action-certified record for a post once,
before its durability vote, and the author sends the certified record of the
head its post extends once, before its proposal. Members no longer send a copy
of every certified record they assemble. A post costs `2 + 3n` outer messages
instead of `1 + 4n`. Measured in the certification harness with sealed
bodies, the bytes each member receives per post fall by 34% at 4 members and
56% at 32 members.
