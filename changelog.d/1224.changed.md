A POST record now carries the `MembershipHash` of its conversation's
membership instead of the full membership descriptor; GENESIS keeps the
descriptor, and a member verifies a POST record against the membership it holds
from GENESIS. This changes record hashes under the same unreleased wire version
`2026.1006.1`. The endpoint store moves to schema 5, and a schema 4 store, like
schema 2 and 3 stores, opens empty and unregistered. Measured in the
certification harness with sealed bodies, the bytes each member receives per
post fall by another 17% at 4 members and 34% at 32 members: 45% and 71%
fewer than before #1221.
