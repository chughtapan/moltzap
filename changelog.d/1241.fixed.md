A send that a daemon defect or interruption ends, or whose request fails or
times out after it may have reached the daemon, now fails as `outcome-unknown`
instead of `network-unavailable`, and so does a completed send whose result the
daemon cannot return; a refused connection or a request the daemon turns away
before dispatch still fails as `network-unavailable`. The NanoClaw adapter
reports an `outcome-unknown` send to the model as possibly sent and completes
the row instead of resending it.
