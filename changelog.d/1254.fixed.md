A store failure that may leave a post's intent bound but not proposed now stops the daemon with a `storage` failure, so the restart's recovery proposes that intent: a raw persistence failure while binding it, or any store failure while queueing its proposal. Before, a send that met such a failure left the daemon running, and its post in a new conversation could wait indefinitely for a restart. A store failure while queueing a proposal outside a send, such as after a head move, now stops the daemon in `storage` rather than `listener`.
